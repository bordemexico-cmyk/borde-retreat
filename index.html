What is BORDÉ?

BORDÉ is a 7-day cultural retreat designed for those who seek to connect deeply with Mexico—not as tourists, but as students, collaborators, and learners of ancestral wisdom.

Facilitated by Katia, this retreat takes you to authentic encounters with indigenous artisans, local archaeologists, and guardians of traditions that have endured centuries. You'll learn about cochineal dye—that red pigment that colored Mexico's history—, traditional textiles, ancestral embroidery techniques, and the worldview of communities who have kept this heritage alive.

It's not an intensive workshop or an art class. It's a journey inward, where each activity—from climbing the pyramids of Monte Albán to learning to embroider with thread as a tool of expression—contributes to your understanding of real Mexico.

Our Commitment: Sustainable & Regenerative Travel

🌿 Sustainable

Every dollar you invest goes directly to artisans, local guides, cooks, and indigenous communities. It's not mass tourism—it's direct support to enterprises and ancestral knowledge.

📚 Mutual Learning

🎨 Authenticity

Work directly with Pastora in her cochineal farm, with Carlos in his embroidery workshop, with Trini in her kitchen. These aren't "demonstrations for tourists"—they're encounters with artisans dedicated to their craft.

🔄 Transformation

This retreat isn't about escape. It's about returning changed. Understanding Mexico's roots means understanding yourself, your own place in community, and what truly connects us.

About Katia

I'm Katia Herrera, a Mexican industrial designer, photographer, and the artist behind BORDÉ.

For over six years, I have combined photography, embroidery, and design to transform meaningful images into one-of-a-kind works of art. By carefully stitching directly onto printed photographs, I add color, texture, and dimension while preserving the story and emotion behind each memory.

Through BORDÉ by Katia, I create custom embroidered photographs, teach embroidery on paper through online courses and workshops, and design templates and kits for anyone who wants to explore this unique art form.

Inspired by my Mexican heritage, my work celebrates color, craftsmanship, human connection, and the beauty of transforming personal memories into art.

Your 7-Day Journey

Day 1 — Thursday, February 25

Arrival & Welcome

Morning & Afternoon: Airport transfers (self-coordinated). Check-in at Luna de Jade.

Evening: Welcome dinner with Katia and fellow travelers. Meet your retreat community, learn about the days ahead, and begin to shift your mind toward cultural immersion.

Day 2 — Friday, February 26

Ancient Roots: Monte Albán

Morning: Breakfast at the hotel (until 8 AM). Early departure to Monte Albán.

Day: Walking tour of the Monte Albán archaeological site with Luis, our English-speaking local guide. Explore the pyramids and ruins of this pre-Hispanic capital, learning about the Zapotec civilization and the layers of history beneath your feet. This is where you begin to understand Mexico's roots—literally.

Afternoon: Return to hotel for rest. One meal free for you to decide your choice.

Day 3 — Saturday, February 27

Threads & Cochineal: Ancient Dyes

Morning: Visit to the cochineal dye farm. Learn the story of grana cochinilla—the red pigment that colored Mexico's conquest and trade history. See how indigenous communities have cultivated this tiny insect for centuries.

Midday: Natural dyes workshop with Pastora. Create your own dyes using traditional methods and natural materials. Understand how color—and commerce—shaped this region.

Afternoon: Meal with Trini, our culinary guide. Taste authentic Oaxacan cuisine prepared with knowledge and care. Learn how food is culture.

Late Afternoon: Embroidery workshop with Carlos. Begin learning ancestral embroidery techniques. Your hands will begin to understand what centuries of craftspeople have known.

Evening: Dinner at La Otra Popular, a local favorite.

Day 4 — Sunday, February 28

The Market & Threads

Morning: Visit to Hilos Flojos, where master weavers and embroiderers share their workspace and stories.

Midday: Café visit and exploration of Mercado La Merced area with a local guide. Navigate the market as a participant, not a tourist—understanding context, relationships, and what artisans create.

Afternoon: Traditional embroidery workshop (continued). Your skills deepen. Your connection to the craft deepens.

Evening: Dinner at Zandunga, a storied restaurant with deep roots in Oaxacan culture.

Day 5 — Monday, March 1

Katia's Embroidery Intensive & Shopping

Morning & Afternoon: Katia's Master Class in embroidery (3 hours, intensive). This is where everything converges—technique, story, art, and personal expression. Learn from Katia herself as she shares her unique approach to transforming thread into meaning.

Late Afternoon: Guided shopping time with context. Visit artisans you've met. Purchase pieces with understanding of the hands that made them, the stories behind them, the communities you're supporting.

Evening: Closing dinner celebration at Levadura de Olla. Reflect on your transformation with your new community.

Day 6 — Saturday, March 3

Departure

Morning: Breakfast at the hotel. Final goodbyes to Katia and fellow travelers.

Afternoon: Return transfers to airport (self-coordinated).

You leave changed. That's the point.
