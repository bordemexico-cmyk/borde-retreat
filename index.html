What is BORDÉ?

BORDÉ is a 6-day cultural retreat designed for those who seek to connect deeply with Mexico—not as tourists, but as students, collaborators, and learners of ancestral wisdom.

Facilitated by Katia, this retreat takes you to authentic encounters with indigenous artisans, local archaeologists, and guardians of traditions that have endured centuries. You'll learn about cochineal dye—that red pigment that colored Mexico's history—, traditional textiles, ancestral embroidery techniques, and the worldview of communities who have kept this heritage alive.

It's not an intensive workshop or an art class. It's a journey inward, where each activity—from climbing the pyramids of Monte Albán to learning to embroider with thread as a tool of expression—contributes to your understanding of real Mexico.

Our Commitment: Sustainable & Regenerative Travel

🌿 Sustainable

Every dollar you invest goes directly to artisans, local guides, cooks, and indigenous communities. It's not mass tourism—it's direct support to enterprises and ancestral knowledge.

📚 Mutual Learning

We don't come to teach. We come to learn. Our small group (maximum 13 people) allows genuine conversations, real questions, and relationships that transcend transactional tourism.

🎨 Authenticity

Work directly with Pastora in her cochineal farm, with Carlos in his embroidery workshop, with Trini in her kitchen. These aren't "demonstrations for tourists"—they're encounters with artisans dedicated to their craft.

🔄 Transformation

This retreat isn't about escape. It's about returning changed. Understanding Mexico's roots means understanding yourself, your own place in community, and what truly connects us.

About Katia

I'm Katia Herrera, a Mexican industrial designer, photographer, and the artist behind BORDÉ.

For over six years, I have combined photography, embroidery, and design to transform meaningful images into one-of-a-kind works of art. By carefully stitching directly onto printed photographs, I add color, texture, and dimension while preserving the story and emotion behind each memory.

Through BORDÉ by Katia, I create custom embroidered photographs, teach embroidery on paper through online courses and workshops, and design templates and kits for anyone who wants to explore this unique art form.

Inspired by my Mexican heritage, my work celebrates color, craftsmanship, human connection, and the beauty of transforming personal memories into art.
